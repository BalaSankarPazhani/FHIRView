Why can't I enter any server?

This page carries a Content-Security-Policy that lets the browser talk only to challenge.fhir.medblocks.com and hapi.fhir.org. That is what makes it safe to paste a token here: even a bug in this page could not send it anywhere else. To use another server, fork the repository and add it to the policy in index.html.
